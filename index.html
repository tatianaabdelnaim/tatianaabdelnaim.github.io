Hello, a bit about me:

I am in my final year and currently on the academic job market. I am working under the supervision of Dr. Peter Patzt . I received my B.S. in Mathematics from the Lebanese University and my M.S. from the American University of Beirut.

Research Interests

  • Group Cohomology
  • Congruence subgroups of arithmetic groups
  • Steinberg module
  • Homological Stability

Research Papers

  • Double Steinberg coinvariants for special linear groups, with David Chan, Alexander Kupers, Robin J. Sroka, Matthew Scalamandre arxiv: 2609.39991
  • Codimensions one and two cohomology of Hecke congruence subgroups, with Jeremy Miller arxiv: 2606.23519
  • Top-dimensional rational cohomology of the congruence subgroup Γ0,n(p) arxiv: 2605.23526

Outreach Highlights
